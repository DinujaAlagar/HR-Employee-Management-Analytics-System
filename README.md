# HR Employee Management & Analytics System

> A web-based Human Resource Management System designed to centralize employee information and streamline attendance, leave, performance, and HR analytics processes.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Employee Management & Analytics System** is a web-based HRM solution developed to support essential human resource activities through a centralized digital platform.

The system enables HR personnel to manage employee records, monitor attendance, process leave requests, evaluate employee performance, and analyze HR-related data.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Centralize employee information
- Improve attendance management
- Simplify leave administration
- Support structured performance evaluation
- Provide HR analytics for decision-making
- Reduce manual HR record-keeping
- Demonstrate the application of technology in HRM

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👥 Employee Management
- Employee records
- Department and position information
- Employment status tracking

### 🕒 Attendance Management
- Daily attendance records
- Check-in and check-out tracking
- Present and late status monitoring

### 🏖️ Leave Management
- Leave request management
- Leave type and date tracking
- Approval status monitoring

### 📊 Performance Management
- Communication
- Teamwork
- Productivity
- Leadership
- Problem-solving
- Overall performance score

### 📈 HR Analytics
- Department-wise analysis
- Attendance analysis
- Performance analysis
- Visual HR dashboards
